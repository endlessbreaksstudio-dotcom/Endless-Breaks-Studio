Ocean Memories

Everyone has an ocean memory that stays with them: a trip, a swim, a day with the people they love. Those memories don't fade, even when the place or the people are far away. Endless Breaks Studio pours that feeling into resin: each piece starts as a wave, built up in tinted layers by hand until the curl, the whitewash, and the deep water underneath are frozen in a single slab.

It's not just decoration. It's something to hold onto.

What I create

Every piece starts the same way, as a wave, then becomes whatever holds it best. Each one is built on a textured acrylic base with a resin pour, so no two ever come out quite the same.

How it's made

Shape

A wood base is cut to form: a board, a tray, a cutting board, ready to become the canvas for a wave.

Pour

Resin is tinted in ocean tones and built up in layers by hand until the curl and whitewash take shape.

Finish

Every piece is sanded and sealed smooth, ready to use, hang, or hand to someone who'll know exactly why.
